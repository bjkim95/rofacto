You will be given a sequence of frames sampled uniformly from a short video clip of a robotic manipulation scene captured by an externally mounted (third-person) camera. Treat the frames as a static snapshot of the scene — do NOT describe any motion, action, or interaction.

Your task: produce a SINGLE natural-language description of the scene as it appears, with rich detail about objects and their physical properties.

Cover, in this order, in a flowing paragraph (no bullet points, no JSON):
1. Camera viewpoint, framing, lighting (indoor/outdoor, color temperature, shadow quality).
2. Static scene: workspace surface (material, color, texture), background elements, surrounding furniture or fixtures, and their relative spatial layout.
3. Objects on or around the workspace: for each object, state its identity, color, material, rigidity class (rigid / deformable / liquid / granular), approximate size, and position relative to the workspace and to other objects. For any object in close proximity to where interaction might occur, provide extra detail: exact color shade, surface texture, estimated dimensions, and any distinguishing features (labels, logos, markings, transparency). Describe object properties only — do NOT state or imply what any agent will do with any object.

Style:
- Present tense.
- Concrete and visual; no verbs implying motion or intent (no reach / extend toward / approach / interact / operate / grasp / hold / touch).
- 120 to 160 words.
- One paragraph. No headings, no list markers, no JSON.
